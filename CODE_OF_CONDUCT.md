# Code of Conduct

Sumplus follows the Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct.

## Our Standard

Project participants are expected to communicate respectfully, focus on the work,
and avoid harassment, threats, discriminatory language, or disclosure of private
information.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove comments, issues, pull requests, or contributors that do
not meet this standard. Serious concerns should be reported privately to the
Sumplus maintainers.
